Why: `client.ts` is the single point of backend contact ("components must never call

one `error` frame then a server-side close — an unbounded loop would hammer the endpoint,
the same failure `PipelineView` already guards against by unsubscribing on terminal states.
Affects: `frontend/src/components/Chat.tsx`.

a single consumer's benefit and duplicate policy the component is better placed to own;
unbounded retry.
