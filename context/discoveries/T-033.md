## OPEN — 2026-08-19 [pipeline-agent (T-033) → pipeline-agent (whoever adds the first consumer of the divergence flag)]
**`_DIVERGENCE_THRESHOLD = 0.05` is an absolute difference in the metric's own units, and is
therefore scale-dependent.** `kaggle_client` flags CV/LB divergence with
`abs(divergence) > 0.05`, where `divergence` is a plain subtraction of two scores in the
competition metric's native scale. For an RMSE in the thousands the flag can never fire; for a
metric whose whole range is smaller than 0.05 it always fires. It is not normalized by the score
magnitude, the metric's range, or the baseline.
This was accepted for v1 by explicit Orchestrator ruling: the flag is advisory, nothing reads it
yet, and the artifact was pinned at exactly nine keys — so shipping the simple version costs
nothing today. It is documented as scale-dependent in the module docstring and in
`docs/pipeline.md`'s Phase 7 section.
Required action: when the first real consumer of `divergence_flag` lands, replace the absolute
threshold with a metric-aware or relative rule (e.g. relative to `abs(cv_score)`, or to the
baseline-to-best span), and add whatever artifact key that rule needs to be auditable.
Task where this should be addressed: unassigned
